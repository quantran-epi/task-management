Everything you need to ship

Built for developers and solo builders who want realistic plans, not optimistic guesses.

Workload Forecasting

See whether your planned work actually fits your available hours. Overload surfaces before deadlines, not after.

Daily Capacity Planner

Allocate hours per task per day. TaskMate suggests feasible distributions based on your rules and deadlines.

Projects & Milestones

Organize work into projects with milestones and deadlines. Track progress with real completion data, not guesses.

Task Management

Create, prioritize, estimate, and track tasks with custom fields, recurring schedules, and time logging built in.

100% Private & Offline

Your data lives on your machine. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Works fully offline with encrypted backup option.

AI-Powered Insights

Built-in AI assistant for task analysis, estimation suggestions, and intelligent project insights — all processed locally.

Your data stays yours

TaskMate runs entirely on your desktop. No server, no signup, no telemetry. Optional encrypted backups go to your own GitHub repo — encrypted with your passphrase before they leave the app.

  • Zero network requests in normal operation
  • AES-256 encrypted backups
  • Passphrase never stored
  • Open source — audit the code yourself

| T-19.2-OPS-02 | Denial of Service | Kubernetes readiness probe | mitigate | Separate fast liveness from authenticated internal readiness; cache status to prevent probe thrashing against Neo4j. |

| T-19.2-OPS-04 | Information Disclosure | Smoke CLI evidence artifact | mitigate | Strict metadata-only schema for CLI results (latencies, counts, safe IDs); automated test asserts zero query or excerpt text in JSON. |

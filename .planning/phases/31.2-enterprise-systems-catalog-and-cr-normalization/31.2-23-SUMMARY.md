# Phase 31.2 Plan 23: Notes, Wizard Services and SDLC Steps off Project/Milestone Summary

Re-axed the notes family, the wizard service family, and the two remaining SDLC step surfaces onto CR/System, leaving `src/` with no `db.projects`/`db.milestones` read outside `src/workbench/EditorArea.tsx` (owned by plans 05/06/13).

## What changed

**Task 1 — notes, diagrams, markdown.** `MermaidAssistantView.tsx` and `NotesPopoutView.tsx` already read `db.changeRequests` / `db.systems` from plan 05, so only their consumers needed work. `DocListPane`'s entity filter is now `'all' | 'standalone' | 'task' | 'cr' | 'system'` with `CR` / `Hệ thống` options; `DocFolderTree` renders a `cr` badge (`ThunderboltOutlined`) and a new `system` badge (`BranchesOutlined`); `SmartIngestionBanner` maps `cr`/`system` icons; `EntityDiagramsSection` labels `CR` / `Hệ thống`; `markdown.ts` widens the wiki-link alternation to `(doc|task|cr|system)` while keeping the `data-entity-type` contract and `escapeHtml` ordering. `NotesView` drops `onOpenProject` and the `#/projects` hash write, opening a `cr_detail` tab via `openTab` instead, and its permanent-delete copy reads `Tasks/CRs` off `backlinks.changeRequests`.

**Task 2 — wizard service family.** `projectId` became `changeRequestId` across eleven files (types, params, object literals, Zod fields, inferred types). `artifactRepo` writes `entityType: 'cr'` and resolves artifacts against CR-scoped threads; `WRONG_PROJECT` became `WRONG_CHANGE_REQUEST`. Every persisted key literal is byte-identical: `workflowStorageKey` still emits `` `sdlc_workflow:${changeRequestId}:${crId}` `` and the step thread keys still emit `` `sdlc:${changeRequestId}:${crId}:${STEP}` ``.

**Task 3 — SDLC step surfaces.** `Step5TechDocsView` renamed its prop and kept its thread-key literal unchanged. `CrCreateModal` now calls `createChangeRequest({ code, name, type, status: 'Open', targets }, db)`, drops the `createNewCr` import and the legacy settings-store prop, and takes an optional `initialTargets` so a subsystem-scoped open becomes the CR's target.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `chatRepo` and `validateBackup` read the renamed fields**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** Renaming `WizardMetadata.projectId` and `SdlcArtifactMetadata.projectId` broke `src/db/repositories/chatRepo.ts:145` and six reads in `src/services/backup/validateBackup.ts` (TS2339 on every one).
- **Fix:** Followed the rename in both files and updated the two stale comments that described the field as "still named projectId until a later wave".
- **Files modified:** `src/db/repositories/chatRepo.ts`, `src/services/backup/validateBackup.ts`
- **Commit:** `01efcf7a`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] `workflowCoordinator` wrote a field the strict schema rejects**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The blanket rename turned `SdlcWorkflowState`'s persisted `projectId` write into `changeRequestId`, but `SdlcWorkflowStateSchema` is a `z.strictObject` still declaring `projectId` (plan 17's file). Every `emit()` would have thrown `Unrecognized key: "projectId"` — verified by `src/services/wizard/__tests__/hubCrSummary.test.ts` failing 16/17.
- **Fix:** Kept the persisted field name and renamed only the coordinator *dependency* (`deps.changeRequestId`), with a comment naming the owning plan. `workflowStorageKey`'s template literal was already correct.
- **Files modified:** `src/services/wizard/workflowCoordinator.ts`
- **Commit:** `01efcf7a`

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `ChangeRequestSdlcView` call sites for the renamed inputs**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** The view passed `projectId` into `createSdlcWorkflowCoordinator` and `collectEvidence`, both renamed in Task 2 — two TS2353 errors blocking the build.
- **Fix:** Passed `changeRequestId` at both call sites and renamed the `Step5TechDocsView` prop at its call site, as the plan instructed.
- **Files modified:** `src/views/ChangeRequestSdlcView.tsx`
- **Commit:** `325c8167`

<assumption_delta_decision>
- **Primary noun:** `AiTeam` remains the owner of the run-concurrency *setting*. The change is that a constant became a chosen value; the anchor did not move.
- **Decision:** `no-change`.
- **Rationale:** D-01 scopes the limit per team and D-03 persists it on the team row; D-08 forbids the scheduler from holding its own assignment list, so the scheduler must derive its limit from that single persisted noun rather than store a second copy. The scheduler instance is runtime state, not an identity model.
- **Accepted debt / promote trigger:** a global cap, or a per-run override, would make the run (or the app) the owning noun and force a promote — the scheduler would need an additional acquire layer and the batch row a `limitAtStart` snapshot. Neither is in scope (CONTEXT Deferred).
</assumption_delta_decision>

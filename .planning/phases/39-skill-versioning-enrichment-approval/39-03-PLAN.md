<objective>
Make the pin real: write the approved version number onto the attempt row at creation, carry it into the tool context, and have `read_skill` resolve that exact version.

Purpose: criterion 5 and Pitfall 18 are the same requirement — writing a number while still reading `aiSkills` is a trace, not a pin. This plan is what makes a mid-batch approval safe to click, and it must land before any approval UI exists.

Output: `TeamToolContext.skillVersion`, `AttemptRef.skillVersion`, a `pinnedSkillVersion` map in the coordinator, pin computation at the dispatch site, recomputation at the retry site, inheritance at the amend site, and a `readSkill` that resolves by exact version with an approved-only guard and a baseline fallback.
</objective>

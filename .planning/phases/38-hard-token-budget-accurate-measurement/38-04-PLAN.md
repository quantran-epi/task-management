<objective>
Fix the budget-write paths end to end: one additive two-table top-up, and one absolute two-table settings save that cannot double-count.

Purpose: AIO-BUDGET-07 fails today in both directions — the agent scope raises a member share without raising the group total, and the team scope raises the total without touching a single member (`teamRunCoordinator.ts:3198-3211`). WR-13 adds a third failure: `ensureMembers` early-returns on `membersLoaded`, so a member added mid-turn is invisible to `topUp` forever. And the Settings save composes an absolute `updateTeam(total)` with a per-member `topUp` loop, so a total of 3000 raised to 4000 becomes 5000 (`TeamWorkspace.tsx:685-718`). All four are one bug class: budget state written in more than one place, from a stale cache, with an ambiguous verb.
Output: `applyTopUp` and `applyTeamBudgetSettings` in the repo, a coordinator `topUp` that delegates with a fresh roster, a `handleSaveSettings` that makes one call, and tests that prove two-table atomicity, mid-turn membership and the 3000→4000 case.
</objective>

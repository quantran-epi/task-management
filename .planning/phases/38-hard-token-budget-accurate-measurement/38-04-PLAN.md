<objective>
Fix the top-up path end to end: one transaction over member and team rows, a fresh roster read, and a team-scope top-up that actually reaches the members.

Purpose: AIO-BUDGET-07 fails today in both directions — the agent scope raises a member share without raising the group total, and the team scope raises the total without touching a single member (`teamRunCoordinator.ts:3198-3211`). WR-13 adds a third failure: `ensureMembers` early-returns on `membersLoaded`, so a member added mid-turn is invisible to `topUp` forever. All three are one bug class: budget state written in more than one place, from a stale cache.
Output: `applyTopUp` in the repo, `topUp` in the coordinator delegating to it with a fresh roster, and tests that prove the two-table atomicity and the mid-turn member case.
</objective>

<objective>
Turn the budget system from a gate into pure threshold math, and represent the threshold as an optional per-team override resolved against a global default.

Purpose: D-01/D-02 abolish the blocking gate; D-04/D-05 replace the four hard-coded `0.8` sites with one resolved percentage. The override must stay distinguishable from the effective value, or a team that inherits global 65 silently becomes an explicit 80 the next time any unrelated Settings field is saved. This plan delivers the shared math, the coordinator-side resolution with a clearable override, the snapshot shape, and one shared test stub.
Output: `budgetGate.ts` as a warning-only math module, a snapshot carrying both override and effective threshold, a shared snapshot test helper, and `budgetGate.test.ts` rewritten around percentages.
</objective>

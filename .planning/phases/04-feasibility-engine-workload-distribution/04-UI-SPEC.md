## Visual Hierarchy & Focal Points

1. **Primary Visual Anchor**: Feasibility Result Banner (`Alert` card at top of modal results). Immediately communicates feasibility status (green Feasible vs warning/red Infeasible), key surplus/deficit metric, and actionable resolution buttons (`Extend to {date}`, `Allocate Available`).
2. **Secondary Interactive Anchor**: Candidate Allocations Table. Draws user focus to proposed daily distribution with editable minute inputs, live allocation summary footer (`Total Proposed` vs `Remaining Estimate`), and day status indicators.
3. **Tertiary Supporting Elements**: Scope & Parameters control bar (date range and distribution strategy segmented control) and collapsible Date Inspection Breakdown panel.

## Component Inventory & Interactions

### 1. `FeasibilityModal`
- **Trigger**: "✨ Check Feasibility & Auto-Distribute" button in `TaskDrawerPlanning` and "+ Auto-Distribute" toolbar action in `PlannerView`.
- **Width**: `720px` responsive (full screen width on mobile `< 768px`).
- **Header**: Icon `<ThunderboltOutlined />` + Title `Task Feasibility & Workload Distribution`.
- **Section 1: Scope & Parameters**:
  - Evaluation Window: Mode toggle (`By Deadline` vs `Custom Range`).
  - Strategy selector: `Segmented` control with options:
    - `Balanced Spread` (Adheres to CALC-05, balances load across days, earlier dates break ties).
    - `Front-load` (Packs capacity from earliest eligible date forward).
    - `Greedy Fill` (Fills lowest-load days to capacity to minimize working days).
  - Max hours/day: Optional `InputNumber` cap (default: unbounded up to net balance).
- **Section 2: Feasibility Result Banner (CALC-03)**:
  - If **Feasible**: Green `Alert` with `<CheckCircleOutlined />`: "Feasible: Work fits within range. Surplus capacity: +Xh Ym."
  - If **Infeasible**: Warning/Error `Alert` with `<ExclamationCircleOutlined />`: "Infeasible: Deficit of -Xh Ym. Earliest feasible date: {date}."
    - Action buttons in alert: `[Extend to {date}]` and `[Allocate Available (Xh Ym)]`.
- **Section 3: Candidate Allocations Table (CALC-05, CALC-06, D-15)**:
  - Columns: Checkbox (Include/Exclude), Date (`YYYY-MM-DD (ddd)`), Existing Task Allocation (`Xh Ym` or `-`), Proposed Allocation (`InputNumber` with presets + step 15m), Resulting Total (`Xh Ym`), Day Status (`Available` / `Busy`).
  - Live summary footer: `Total Proposed: Xh Ym / Task Remaining Estimate: Zh Wm`.
- **Section 4: Date Inspection Breakdown (CALC-04, D-11)**:
  - Collapsible panel `Inspect Date Details ({count} days evaluated)`.
  - Summary metric tags: `{N} Available`, `{N} Full`, `{N} Overloaded`, `{N} Excluded (Weekend/Holiday)`.
  - Compact table listing each date, day capacity, active load, net balance, and eligibility badge.
- **Footer**:
  - `Discard Allocations` button (dismisses modal preview without persisting changes, restores focus to trigger).
  - `Apply Allocations` primary button (commits allocations to Dexie within an atomic transaction, shows Ant Design success notification, and closes modal).

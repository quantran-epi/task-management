## Phase Goal

**As a** solo planner, **I want to** evaluate task estimates against capacity and preview deterministic lowest-load distributions, **so that** I know before scheduling whether my work fits available time and can commit allocations with one click.

<objective>
Build the pure TypeScript feasibility evaluation engine and workload distribution algorithms. The engine evaluates unallocated task estimates against inclusive date ranges or deadlines, accounts for weekly capacity templates, specific date overrides, zero-capacity non-working days, and existing active allocations. It produces clear surplus/deficit metrics, calculates the earliest feasible date, and generates deterministic candidate distributions (Balanced Spread, Front-load, Greedy Fill) with 15-minute quantization.

Purpose: Provide a decoupled, pure algorithmic foundation with 100% test coverage before building the UI layer.
Output: `src/types/feasibility.ts`, `src/utils/feasibility.ts`, `tests/utils/feasibility.test.ts`.
</objective>

<objective>
Convert `delegate_batch` from blocking to event-driven (D-24): the leader dispatches, its turn ends, agents run in the background, and a settle event opens a NEW lead turn to synthesize. This is the foundation every other 37.1 plan builds on — without it the leader is busy for the whole batch and steering needs a second lead runtime, which the user rejected.

Purpose: the leader must be free while agents work so a mid-batch steer can open a real lead turn (SC1).
Output: `dispatchBatch` + `onBatchSettled` in `teamRunCoordinator.ts`, a batch-scoped `AbortController`, a per-turn usage baseline, a `teamTurnId` that survives the batch, and the reworked `delegate_batch` test contract.
</objective>
